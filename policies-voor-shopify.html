Retour- en terugbetalingsbeleid ← Returns Policy

At Sterling & Co. Melbourne, a family-run boutique led by Margaret and her daughter Charlotte, we do everything we can to keep our customers happy. Still, there may be times you'd like to return a product. Below, we've clearly explained our returns policy. We're happy to help — if you have any questions, please contact us first at support@shopsterlingco.com.

1. How we operate

Sterling & Co. Melbourne works with a direct-from-supplier shipping model. To reduce waste and limit our carbon footprint, we don't hold large stock ourselves: items are shipped directly from our supplier when not already available locally. This means returns also need to be sent to our supplier's return address.

2. Return window and request

You can request a return within 30 days of receiving your item by emailing support@shopsterlingco.com.

You can only request a return after you have physically received the item.

Please always include your order number and a valid reason for the return in your request.

Once approved, we'll send you the return address to use.

3. Conditions

A valid reason for return is always required.

Items must be unused, unwashed, undamaged, and in their original condition, with all tags still attached.

You're welcome to try items on, but not wear them.

Sale items can't be returned without a valid reason.

Knowingly ordering multiple sizes with the intention of returning some of them is not permitted.

Certain items are excluded from return for hygiene reasons (such as underwear, swimwear, and jewellery), unless they are still sealed and unused.

4. Return costs and process

Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility, including international shipping and any customs charges that may apply.

The item must be sent back to our supplier's return address.

You are responsible for the shipping, related costs, and tracking of your return.

We always recommend using a trackable shipping method — without proof of postage, we're unable to process the return.

5. Alternative solution

Because international returns can be costly and time-consuming, we're happy to discuss an alternative solution with you (such as a partial refund or a discount), to be agreed by email.

We always encourage you to contact us first at support@shopsterlingco.com before returning any item.

6. Faulty, incorrect, or damaged items

If something isn't right with your order — for example the size, fit, or quality — please email us within 30 days of receiving your item with clear photos and a detailed description of the issue. Without photos and a description, we're unable to process the claim and no right to a return or refund applies. With this information, we'll work to find a suitable solution as quickly as possible.

7. Refunds

A refund can only be issued once the item has been received and approved at our supplier's return address. Once approved, we'll refund your purchase within 14 business days using your original payment method. Original shipping costs are non-refundable.

8. Cancellations

Orders can't be cancelled once they have been placed and entered processing. A refund request or chargeback can't be opened before the item has actually arrived and been received. If you have a question or issue with your order, please contact us first at support@shopsterlingco.com rather than opening a refund request or chargeback straight away.

9. Liability

We're not responsible for lost parcels where tracking shows the item was delivered to the address provided by the customer.

An incorrectly entered address is the customer's responsibility.

Any address errors must be reported within 24 hours of placing the order by emailing support@shopsterlingco.com.

Nothing in this policy limits any consumer guarantee you have under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot be excluded.

Privacybeleid ← Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all services provided by Sterling & Co. Melbourne, which can be contacted at support@shopsterlingco.com. We respect the privacy of our customers and handle the personal information you provide to us in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. What data we collect

We collect the personal data you provide when you place an order, create an account, or contact our customer service team, including:

  • Full name
  • Shipping address and billing details
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Payment details (processed by our payment providers and not stored on our servers)
  • IP address and device/browser data
  • Order and transaction history
  • Data about your activity and behaviour on our website (via cookies)

2. How we use your data

  • To process, ship, and invoice your orders.
  • To provide customer service and support.
  • To send order updates, tracking information, and relevant communications.
  • To improve our services, website, and product range.
  • To prevent and detect fraud and misuse.
  • To send marketing communications, but only where you have given your consent.

3. Legal basis for processing

We process your data on the basis of the performance of a contract (your order), your consent (for example, for marketing), a legal obligation (for example, for tax purposes), and our legitimate interests (for example, fraud prevention and improving our services).

4. Sharing data with third parties

Your data is only shared with third parties to the extent necessary to deliver our services, including:

  • Our payment service providers.
  • Our shipping and delivery partners.
  • Our suppliers, for the purpose of processing and shipping your order.
  • Analytics and marketing service providers (where applicable and in line with your cookie preferences).

Data processing agreements are in place with all these parties where required. We never sell your data to third parties. As some of our orders are shipped directly by a supplier located outside Australia, your data may be transferred overseas; where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, consistent with Australian Privacy Principle 8.

5. Retention period

We keep your data for no longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or for as long as required by law (for example, a statutory record-keeping obligation).

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve functionality, analyse visitor behaviour, and support marketing activities. Please see our Cookie Policy for more information. You can always change your preferences via the cookie banner or your browser settings.

7. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. All payments are processed via a secure SSL connection.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, correct it, request its deletion, or restrict its processing. You may also object to processing, withdraw your consent, and request data portability where applicable.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@shopsterlingco.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Verzendbeleid ← Shipping & Delivery

We're committed to delivering your order as quickly and accurately as possible. Below you'll find everything you need to know about our shipping and delivery policy.

1. Processing time (dispatch)

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days. Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a tracking code by email.

2. Delivery times

After processing, average shipping time is 7–14 business days, depending on your location. Please note delivery may be delayed during periods of high demand, public holidays, or due to customs processing.

3. Shipping method and origin

To reduce waste and our carbon footprint, we ship directly from our supplier. As a result, depending on your delivery location, customs charges may apply. Your order is delivered using a combination of trusted international couriers and, within Australia, local postal and courier partners.

4. Shipping costs

Shipping costs are shown at checkout and depend on weight, destination, and the shipping method selected.

5. Tracking your shipment — customer responsibility

You're responsible for monitoring your tracking code via our website and the link provided by email.

You're also responsible for checking whether your parcel will be delivered to your home or to a pickup point, and for collecting it yourself if required.

If delivery is taking longer than expected or an issue arises, please always email us first at support@shopsterlingco.com rather than opening a chargeback or refund request straight away.

6. Liability during shipping

If tracking information shows the parcel has been delivered, responsibility passes to the customer.

Please double-check your address carefully; any errors must be reported within 24 hours of placing your order by emailing support@shopsterlingco.com.
